The Beagle

The Beagle is a breed of small scent hound, similar in appearance to the much larger foxhound. They are known for their great sense of smell and their friendly, curious nature.

Beagle Trivia

  1. Excellent Sense of Smell: Beagles have around 220 million scent receptors.
  2. Vocal Dogs: The word "Beagle" may come from the French word "begueule," meaning "open throat."
  3. Famous Pop Culture Icons: The world's most famous Beagle is arguably Snoopy from the Peanuts comic strip.
  4. Presidential Pets: U.S. President Lyndon B. Johnson had three Beagles named Him, Her, and Edgar.
